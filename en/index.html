One more source.

ShowAPI (万维易源) provides an ISBN lookup service for titles, authors, publishers, and other book details. Get your own API key (called an appKey on the platform), then add and enable it in My Bookshelf.

  1. 01

    Sign up and get your appKey

    Open the service page above and sign up or sign in to ShowAPI. Choose “查看我的 appKey” (View my appKey) or “查看密钥” (View key), then copy your appKey from the console.

  2. 02

    Add your key and enable it

    In the app, go to “我的 → 书籍信息服务” (Profile → Book information services). Find “万维易源” (ShowAPI), paste your key into “万维易源 appKey”, and turn the service on.

  3. 03

    Test the connection, then save

    Tap “测试 Key” (Test key) to check the connection. Once it succeeds, tap “保存” (Save) in the top-right corner. The service is then ready for ISBN scans and lookups. Testing alone does not save the key.

Quotas and service terms are set by ShowAPI; refer to its official pages. “Test key” makes a real lookup and may use one API call.

Before you begin

A few things to know

About your own little library.

Is this an ebook reader?

My Bookshelf helps you manage your personal collection: book details, reading status, and storage locations. It does not provide ebook content or book file downloads.

What if a book has no ISBN or cannot be found?

Add the title, author, and other details manually; an ISBN is optional. Online results come from third-party sources and may be incomplete. Review them before saving, or edit and add details later.

Can I use it offline?

You can view, search, categorize, and organize saved books offline. Online lookups and remote cover images require an internet connection. Some services need your own API key. Remote cover references are not the same as images saved locally.

How do I move my library to a new phone?

Go to “我的 → 数据导入导出” (Profile → Data import/export) and export a full ZIP backup. Keep it somewhere safe, install the app on your new phone, and restore it there. ZIP includes local images and settings; CSV is better for spreadsheets. Back up regularly and check the preview before restoring.
